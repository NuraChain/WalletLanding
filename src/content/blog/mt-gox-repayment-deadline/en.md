Mt. Gox collapsed in 2014. On 31 October 2026 — the trustee's current deadline, after several extensions — its rehabilitation plan is due to finish paying creditors. As of 30 September, wallets attributed to the estate still held 34,387.51 BTC, about $2.88 billion, according to Arkham data cited by Crypto Briefing. Around 19,500 of an estimated 24,000 creditors have been paid.

Twelve years is long enough that most people holding crypto today were not there for it. It is still the clearest demonstration of what a balance on an exchange is.

## What happened at Mt. Gox?

It was the largest Bitcoin exchange of its time. Customers sent it bitcoin, and it showed them a number. In 2014 it failed, having lost an estimated 850,000 BTC to theft and mismanagement. Roughly 200,000 were later recovered, and those are what the trustee, Nobuaki Kobayashi, has been distributing since.

## Why has repayment taken twelve years?

Because the customers did not own bitcoin. They owned a claim against a company, and when the company failed the claim went where claims go: into a court process, with a trustee, creditor lists, verification, ballots and deadlines. The coins never stopped existing. The people they were owed to had no key to them — only a place in a queue.

## What is the lesson for a wallet today?

Not that exchanges are doomed; most never fail. It is that the failure, when it comes, is a legal event and not a technical one, and you are not a party who can make it go faster. [The question that decides it](/blog/self-custody-explained/) is who holds the private key.

- Keep on an exchange what you are actively trading, and think of it as a deposit.
- Hold the rest under a key you control.
- Then take the other half seriously. With your own key there is no trustee either. [The phrase is the wallet](/blog/recovery-phrase-security/), and nobody can give it back.

Nura is an Ethereum wallet, so it has nothing to hold bitcoin with. The arrangement is the point: the recovery phrase is encrypted on your device, with no account and no server holding a copy. Nobody stands between you and your coins — which also means nobody stands behind you.

Some of the people who trusted Mt. Gox are still waiting. That is what a claim is.
